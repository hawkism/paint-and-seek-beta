    🎲 Rounds

    A match has several rounds. Each round one player is the seeker, and everyone else hides. Sometimes the seeker is you!

    Between rounds you see the scoreboard and can run around the lobby. Tap ✅ I'm ready! (R) or stand on the green pad to go to the map vote, where everyone votes for the next map.

    ⚙️ Difficulty

    Slide Difficulty from 🐣 Very easy to 🔥 Very hard. Each stop sets all of these at once: how smart the computer hiders and seekers are, how long you get to hide, how long you get to find everyone when you seek, how long you must stay hidden when a computer player seeks, the hot-or-cold hint, the second seeker in big games, and how many paint balls you get. The line under the slider says what that stop sets.

    Slide it all the way to ⚙️ Custom to change each one by yourself. Changing any one of them also moves the slider to Custom, and Custom remembers your own set.

    👥 Second seeker: with 5 or 6 computer players, a second computer player seeks too: right away, after 30 seconds, or never.

    🙈 Hider smarts (1–10): how good the computer players are at hiding: painting the right color, picking spots that blend in from every side, sitting down, keeping still and not panicking. Low ones fidget and peek!

    🔍 Seeker smarts (1–10): how fast the computer seekers walk, how far and sharp they see, how often they get a hunch, and how well they throw paint.

    ⭐ Points and 🪙 coins

    Hiders get 120 points for staying hidden the whole round, or fewer points the sooner they are found. Seekers get 30 points for every player they find, plus a bonus for finding everyone. The most points at the end wins the match!

    You also earn coins every round. Spend them in the Shop on hats and paint ball colors.

    🙈 Hiding

    Point the dot at a wall, couch or bush close to you and press PAINT to copy its color, or press 🎨 COLORS (Q) to pick any color you like. Stand right up against something the same color as you.

    Body parts: tap 🧍 All, 🙂 Head, 👕 Body, 💪 Arms or 👖 Legs first (keys 1–5) to paint just that part, so you can be two colors at once, like a red shirt by the couch and brown legs on the wood floor.

    Poses: tap the pose button (X) to Stand, Sit or Lie down. Sitting makes you short enough to hide behind low furniture. Lying down flat is almost invisible if you paint yourself like the floor, but you can only crawl. Jumping stands you back up. Hold still and your eyes close, which makes you even harder to see.

    Listen while you hide: you hear the seekers' footsteps, and your heartbeat gets faster when one comes close (with the sound off, watch for "👣 A seeker is close"). Hold still and let them walk past!

    Watch the seeker: ? means it noticed something, ! means RUN! If paint splats you, press PAINT again to cover the spots. If you get found, you become a seeker too and help find the rest! (You can change this on the menu: "Found players".) When YOU are the seeker, the players you find sit on the bench, so every find is yours. With 5 or more computer players a second computer player seeks too (when depends on the Difficulty). Standing up on a table won't save you: seekers hop up after you!

    🔍 Seeking

    Look for shapes that don't belong, and blinking eyes. Listen for giggles! Press THROW to throw a paint ball. A splatted player gets bright spots and runs away. Bump into a player to tag it.

    👫 Playing together

    Play with friends on other devices: one person taps 👫 Play together and 👑 Host a game, and gets a 4-letter code. Friends type the code and tap Join. The host taps ✅ Allow for each friend, then ▶ Start the match. Computer players fill the other places.

    When a person seeks, they wait with their eyes shut while the others hide. Hiding? Tap ✅ I'm hidden! (R) when you're ready: once everyone has, the seeker starts a few seconds later.

    Only the host can pause. Restarting a round follows the rule the host picked: only the host can restart, or everyone votes and more than half must say 👍. If someone's connection drops, a computer player plays for them until they come back.

    🎮 Controls

    iPad: drag on the left side to move, drag on the right side to look, and tap the big buttons.

    Computer: WASD move, mouse looks (click the game first), Click or E paint/throw, Space jump, P pause, mouse wheel zooms.

    Control type on the menu: Auto switches to touch controls when you touch the screen, and back when you use a mouse. Graphics on Auto is Fast with touch controls and Pretty with a mouse, and turns itself down if the game keeps running slowly.
